Turn Mode Settings

Turn Mode wpml:waypointTurnMode
Controls how the aircraft handles each waypoint. For LiDAR survey lines, use Fly-Through (Continuous) to eliminate stops and maintain data quality.
Turn Damping Distance wpml:waypointTurnDampingDist
How far before the waypoint the aircraft begins its arc (metres). At 20 m/s survey speed, 20–30 m is a good starting point. Larger = wider, earlier arc. Must be less than half the distance between adjacent waypoints.
PRESETS:
Use Straight Line wpml:useStraightLine
When enabled, the segment between waypoints is as straight as possible (the arc only applies near the waypoint). Disable for fully curved trajectories throughout the segment. Keep ON for survey lines.
Enabled — segments fly straight, arc at waypoints only
